Problem Statement Lecturers teach in class while students take notes in real time. In doing so, students often miss points, mishear things, or fail to note anything at all for stretches of the lecture — resulting in incomplete or missing notes that only get discovered as a gap the night before an exam. There is no free, hosted platform that turns a lecture directly into accurate, structured study material available to the whole class immediately after the lecture — and no existing tool gives lecturers feedback on their own delivery (pacing, repeated words, etc.) to improve teaching quality. Classroom environment (confirmed with faculty): These are high-quality classroom setups — not noisy general classrooms. Low echo, Iow background noise, and audio is captured via a mic on the lecturer directly. This meaningfully de-risks the transcription problem compared to a generic noisy-classroom assumption; noise-cleanup is still included as a safety net, but is not the primary technical risk it would be with ambient/laptop-mic recording. 2.
